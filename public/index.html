The group packing list that fills itself in

Local Man Packs Seven Shirts, Forgets Toothbrush

By someone who has done exactly this

Every group trip goes the same way. Somebody posts a list in the chat. The chat moves on. Three days later nobody can find the list, and four people have packed a speaker.

packd fixes that. One person makes the list, everyone else opens a link and ticks things off as they drop them in the bag.

Different people, different lists

Say who you are and it hides the stuff that is not yours. Nobody needs to see "hair dryer" unless they want to.

Please do not all bring the speaker

Mark something as "one person" and the first brave volunteer gets it. Everyone else sees it is handled and goes back to worrying about their own bag.

Sign here

Packed everything? Put your name on it. The front page shows who is ready and who is still sitting on the bed deciding between two jackets.

No accounts

No passwords, no app, no sign up. If you can open a link, you can pack.
